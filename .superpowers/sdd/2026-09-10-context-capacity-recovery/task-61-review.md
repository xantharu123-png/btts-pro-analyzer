#### I1 — Actual active-input allocation and input-directory metadata are not admitted

- **Evidence:** `tests/native_context_receipt_diagnostic.py:381-388` holds each original input and calls the old `file_record`, retaining only logical size/hash. `tests/native_context_receipt_diagnostic_catalogue.py:671-673` checks the 4-GiB envelope by adding rounded declared caps and a declared 128-MiB input-metadata allowance. No input-side measurement of `st_blocks * 512` or aggregate directory allocation establishes that those declared caps cover the actual held inputs. The real `_allocation`/directory measurements are only used for job/registry slots and retained roots, not for this active-input list.
- **Why it matters:** the contract explicitly requires simultaneous *actual admitted inputs* to fit 4 GiB and forbids logical-only/sparse-accounting substitutes. A regular input can have physical preallocation above its logical length; input-directory allocation can also exceed the unmeasured allowance. The generous inclusion of all new slots in the arithmetic is conservative only after the old inputs themselves are shown to fit their assumed physical bounds. Fresh `f_bavail` observes occupied disk but does not prove the separate active-input envelope.
- **Fix:** measure and bind held per-input logical and allocated bytes and the complete bounded input-directory metadata before admission; reject physical slot/aggregate overruns and recheck their epochs/allocation. Preserve explicit input/control membership and avoid double-counting historical occupancy as future growth. Add a focused physical-allocation/metadata-overrun test against this production admission path.
- **Focused unchanged-code risk checked:** old `file_record` might already prove physical bounds. `tests/native_context_chain_catalogue.py:225-315` shows it proves no-follow held identity, single link, logical cap and streamed hash, but returns only `{size, sha256}`; it does not check allocation. This check confirms rather than merely assumes the gap.
