## Global Constraints

- This capture integration performs no additional model fit, score matrix or calibrator invocation. Preserve the exact floats/order of the owning active law, existing ORIGINAL-v1 logical bytes, source clocks and legacy forecasts. The separately versioned coherent-law repair may emit ORIGINAL v2; consume that owning packet without reconstructing its mathematics.
- Price never changes the model pool or sporting provenance. Cricket and monetary state are untouched.
- Missing native links remain explicitly unresolved, never invented. Corruption is not missing data. Later receipts cannot qualify an earlier decision.
- New original storage is transport-only until its owning source/replay/training contract is integrated; no empirical or injury-effect approval is implied.
- No whole-history copy per timestamp. Reuse equal immutable chunks and bodies. No old artifact pruning, migration or rewriting.
- Readers are read-only; freeze physically related bytes in one transaction and release it before CPU decoding. Writers publish complete atomic units with collision/readback checks.
